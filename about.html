UKWELI ARTS
ART / MEMORY / COMMUNITY
UKWELI

Art becomes more than decoration. It becomes memory, identity, dialogue and a reason for people to gather.

The Mau Mau Camp provided space for peace, community meetings, a library, study space, film viewings and a recording studio.

Local artists displayed work on walls and murals, inspiring visitors with pride while environmental activities brought people together to plant trees, grow food and care for their neighbourhood.

Workshops, social-awareness graffiti, entertainment and youth talent showcases transformed the space into a living platform for community building.
